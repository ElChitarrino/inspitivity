# Inspitivity acceptance checklist

## Projects and arrangements

- Create a project with a distinctive title, BPM, root, and mode.
- Reload the app and confirm the project remains.
- Add Verse, Chorus, and Bridge sections; move the Bridge up and down.
- Add a text idea to a specific section and confirm it remains after reload.

## Audio capture

- Allow microphone access, record at least five seconds, then stop and save.
- Confirm the idea appears under the selected section with the correct name and duration.
- Deny microphone access once and confirm the app explains what is needed without losing project data.
- Check on both phone and desktop; microphone formats and permissions differ by browser.

## Chord generation

- Generate simple, medium, and advanced progressions; extensions should become richer.
- Change root, mode, and length and confirm every displayed chord changes consistently.
- Regenerate an individual chord, play once, loop it for at least three cycles, then stop.
- Save the progression and confirm it appears in the intended project section.

## Melody generation

- Generate at least ten melodies with the same settings; compare contour and rhythm, not just the first note.
- Try every mood with the same root, scale, density, and seed sequence; each mood should feel distinct.
- Compare minimum and maximum density; higher density should move faster.
- Confirm every note belongs to the selected scale, stays in range, and resolves to the root.
- Play once, loop for at least three cycles, stop, and save to the project.

## Metronome

- Check 60, 120, and 180 BPM against a trusted metronome for 30 seconds.
- Test 3/4, 4/4, 6/8, and 7/8; the first beat must remain visibly and audibly accented.
- Test each subdivision and use tap tempo with eight steady taps.
- Run the metronome while opening the recorder and confirm the app remains responsive.

## Local-first resilience

- Work offline, reload, and confirm projects and text/generated ideas remain available.
- Download a backup and inspect that project names, metadata, sections, and ideas are present.
- Clear site data only after making a backup; confirm the app returns to starter content.
