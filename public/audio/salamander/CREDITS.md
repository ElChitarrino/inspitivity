# Inspitivity Acoustic Sessions

Modified subset of **Salamander Drumkit**, recorded by **Alexander Holm (Rytmenpinne)**.

Original author and public-domain dedication (4 March 2022):
https://rytmenpinne.wordpress.com/sounds-and-such/salamander-drumkit/

Source mirror: https://github.com/studiorack/salamander-drumkit
The mirror distributes the recordings under CC BY-SA 3.0; its license is included in LICENSE.txt. This modified sample subset is also available under that license. No endorsement is implied.

Preparation: two strengths and two alternate takes for each of eight instruments; leading silence trimmed, half-millisecond onset fade, 40 ms end fade, bounded tails, 0.9 peak ceiling, converted to stereo 44.1 kHz 16-bit PCM. See scripts/prepare-drum-samples.mjs for exact original filenames and processing. No synthetic replacement hits are included.

The acoustic, dry, rock, progressive metal, roomy and dark choices are processing presets of this one recorded kit, not six separately recorded libraries. The original electronic kit remains synthesized.

Playback downloads use 192 kbps stereo MP3 derivatives in `public/audio/packs/`, generated by `scripts/build-audio-packs.mjs`. Original PCM samples remain available above. The derivatives retain the same CC BY-SA 3.0 attribution and license. Core packs contain both strengths; second takes stream after playback becomes ready.
